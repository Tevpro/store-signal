## Assets, trust boundaries, and adversaries

Assets: shopper origin/domain, account and anonymous-install identifiers, account-bound BYOK secrets, provider credentials, evidence/provenance, shared assessments and correction records. Actors: shoppers, store owners, third-party sources/search/Places, AI providers, operators, hostile store operators, coordinated reviewers and attackers who control DNS, redirect responses or snippets. Boundaries: extension → API; API/worker → DNS and network; third-party text → evidence normalization/AI; candidate verdict → public page; account-private BYOK → shared cache; verified owner → privileged refresh; Cloudflare/Node adapters → durable storage. No inbound field or provider response is trusted simply because it arrived through a configured connector. Proposed controls need per-runtime tests; the repo currently only contains plans.

| Threat / path | Required behavior / failure mode | Review evidence before release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSRF via shopper origin, evidence URL, owner-file verification, search result, redirect, or AI override | Parse strict HTTP(S) URL; reject credentials, non-web schemes, ambiguous/obfuscated host forms, non-public IPv4/IPv6 (including loopback, link-local, private, multicast and cloud metadata), unexpected ports; canonicalize and validate DNS A/AAAA at each hop; bind socket/egress to the validated address to avoid DNS rebinding; disable automatic redirects and revalidate every hop with small hop limit. Egress deny private destinations at network layer too. Fail closed, do not fetch or leak target details. OWASP specifically cautions that allowlisting alone does not stop DNS rebinding and recommends binding to validated resolved addresses.[1] | Synthetic redirect chains public→private, IPv6, DNS rebinding and address alias tests; Node and Wrangler-local egress validation; outbound firewall rules reviewed. |
| Unbounded or attacker-controlled fetch | Explicit timeouts, total byte and decompression ceilings, allowed content types, limited concurrency/recursion; no shopper cookies, credentials or internal request headers forwarded. Prefer provider APIs over fetching arbitrary third-party pages. Unsupported/error paths record coverage failure and stop. | Timeout, oversized compressed response, content-type mismatch and no-secret-forwarding tests in both runtimes. |
| Malicious snippets / prompt injection | Treat search snippets, business listings, Reddit content, extension metadata and owner-file bodies as data. Delimit/untrusted-label before model input; cap excerpt size; never let source text provide instructions, tools, destinations, system-role text or secrets. AI emits schema-validated candidate claims referencing existing evidence IDs only; deterministic identity/provenance/independence checks run **outside** the model. Never execute model-generated URLs or override instructions. | Identity and tenant confusion | Server-side normalize store identity; preserve tenant-specific hosted subdomains, reject client-supplied canonical IDs. ## Data minimization and lifecycle (proposal, NOT a final retention schedule)

- Extension sends only normalized store origin on explicit click. Strip path, query, fragment, credentials and tracking tokens **client- and server-side**. No passive history, screenshots, full HTML, user cookies or user↔domain lookup ledger. Domain identity requires tenant-aware normalization; public shared cache contains store-only information. See [requirements](requirements.md#checks-caching-and-privacy).
- Maintain separate stores for public store identity/assessment/provenance, internal investigation/job and refresh history, account/billing data, encrypted BYOK secret, disputes and restricted security logs. Never place account/installation/IP IDs in public evidence or store assessment. A short-lived request-level anti-abuse counter should not persist a domain association. Logs should omit shopper URL paths, query strings, keys, unredacted IP unless strictly justified by reviewed abuse operations, free-text complaints and full third-party bodies.
- Evidence records, **only after source-specific storage and purpose rights are approved**: source URL, provider, retrieval/observation timestamps, identity-match explanation, claim, lineage and necessary licensed excerpt, limited to the exact fields and purposes permitted by the applicable source contract and approved retention policy. Do not retain full review bodies by default. Keep original citation/attribution and source-level usage restrictions. When rights are not established, exclude the evidence from persistence and publication; a URL-only reference is not an automatic exception. Retain identifiers/references only when the exact retained fields and purposes are explicitly permitted by the applicable source contract and approved retention policy. Google Places content has explicit caching and attribution requirements and a place ID exception; legal/provider owner must determine the permitted schema and display before launch.[3]
- Proposed retention trigger, not duration: public dated store assessments live while necessary to display current result/correction, refreshed weekly if actively requested; superseded routine investigation history may be pruned after approved operational window; dispute and audit records retained only for an approved litigation/appeal window; request/security logs and anonymous counters pruned as soon as their reviewed abuse purpose expires; BYOK deleted on authenticated request and account closure with backup expiry documented. **Exact day counts, account/deletion exceptions, processor/subprocessor transfers, IP logs and owner-verification token retention are HUMAN PENDING.** No code may treat a suggested duration as approved until the schedule is signed and implemented with deletion/backup tests.
- Disclose account/managed-AI processor flows separately; BYOK result stays account-private and must not update shared public cache. Document request/erasure route and exceptions in the approved notice; test deletion from primary stores and backup expiry. If compelled retention is needed, counsel decides scope, access and notice.

## Evidence validation and public publication gate

1. Required search and Places connectors configured and healthy at startup in both deployments. For a particular store, `no_match` from a healthy connector is valid; `failed`/`unavailable` is not evidence of safety or wrongdoing. Label search **“indexed Reddit results checked”**, not comprehensive Reddit coverage. When required coverage fails, record the failure privately and do not publish a new public assessment, regardless of candidate concern level. A separately permitted existing dated cache may remain visible only under its own freshness, source-rights and publication rules; it is not a new assessment or evidence that the failed connector was checked.
2. Normalize source/store identity, validate source URL and licensing/display rights, record retrieval and observed dates, provider and limitations. Exact matched GBP rating/review count only; show count/rating/matching rationale/date if used. Reddit allegations must link directly to indexed results and be tied to exact store; multiple reposts of one allegation form one lineage. Independent verification is not inferred from HTTPS, domain age, policies or logos.
3. Candidate Low/Caution/High/Insufficient evidence is a deterministic evidence-based decision. **Low admissibility gate:** when trusted reviews/discussion tied to the exact store are absent, Low is prohibited by default, even if both required connectors are healthy and return `no_match` and no negative evidence is found. Use Caution with reason `limited independent track record` when coverage is adequate to identify that limitation; use Insufficient evidence when coverage is inadequate. The sole exception permits a Low *candidate* without trusted reviews only when independent evidence establishes (a) the business identity was actually verified by a directory/provider that verifies businesses, (b) a proven link between that verified identity and the exact store, and (c) at least one year of independently verifiable operation. Record provider verification method, linked-identity evidence, operating-period evidence and dates for review; mere listing, HTTPS, domain registration age, self-published policies, logos and owner control do not satisfy this gate. The exception is necessary, not an automatic Low: all remaining evidence, coverage and publication checks still apply. Distinguish Caution for limited independent track record vs credible negative evidence. One credible Reddit thread can support Caution, never High without independent corroboration. A GBP rating below 3.0 with at least 20 matched reviews **may** support High after checks; low stars alone are not proof of fraud. High needs validated attributable evidence IDs, current-enough provenance, store match, independence or the specified GBP threshold, and approved display rights. AI interpretation can only propose a candidate; it cannot invent evidence or bypass this gate. The published assessment records decision reason, evidence IDs, coverage and confidence.
4. If any High precondition fails, do **not** publish High: select Caution or Insufficient evidence with a recorded reason based on remaining validated evidence; route dispute or high-impact borderline cases to a human. Never silently upgrade based on cached/bare AI output. Public noindex pages show dates, source links/allowed attribution, uncertainty, connector coverage, limitations and visible correction notices. Noindex is not privacy control or permission to publish unsubstantiated allegations.
5. Until public-claims approval and measured launch benchmark are recorded, **no public High**; stage candidate decisions privately for evaluation. The planned benchmark is ≤5% false High among High outputs and ≥80% of adjudicated risky stores Caution/High on a representative independently labeled set; neither number is presently measured. Owner verification never changes the verdict by itself.

## Proposed public copy for human review — DO NOT PUBLISH

- **High concern**: “We found multiple independently supported signals of concern tied to this store, or a strongly negative rating from a matched business profile with sufficient review volume. Review the dated sources and limitations before deciding. This is not a finding of fraud.” Link the actual supporting sources; when relying on GBP alone, replace “multiple” with the precise matched-rating/count rationale. Do not call a store a scam, criminal, fraudulent or unsafe as fact.
- **Caution / limited track record**: “We could not find enough independently verifiable history to assess this store confidently. Limited evidence is not evidence of wrongdoing.” **Caution / negative evidence**: “We found attributable reports of customer concern; review the linked sources and dates.”
- **Low concern (only after the Low admissibility gate above)**: “We found no material concerns in the checked sources at the stated date and coverage. This is not a guarantee of safety.” Healthy search with no matching trusted reviews alone does **not** authorize this copy. **Insufficient evidence**: “We cannot make a reliable assessment with the available evidence or connector coverage.”
- Scope/attribution: “US-focused, English-language checks. We checked indexed Reddit results, not all Reddit discussion. Google Business Profile information, when shown, is for a matched business and attributed according to provider terms.” Exact Google display language/layout **HUMAN PENDING** with provider rights. Exclude out-of-scope sellers or mark unsupported explicitly.
- Appeal path: each public result has an accessible “Report an error or dispute this assessment” link with store identity and assessment ID prefilled, contact channel, evidence upload instructions and privacy notice. No unverified complainant material or private contact details are republished. Accept reports from shoppers and merchants; verify owner control only for privileged refresh, not to hear a dispute.

## Correction/dispute runbook and proposed SLA (NOT APPROVED)

1. Intake: acknowledge receipt, generate restricted case ID, record page/result revision, complainant contact (separate/private), claimed error, supporting source and timestamp. A credible dispute **immediately flags the public page as under review** with a neutral notice while dated findings remain visible, unless there is clear exposure of private data or unsubstantiated severe allegation; then emergency-hide the specific claim pending review. No automatic favorable re-rating from an owner request.
2. Triage: proposed **one business day** acknowledgement and **two business days** for initial human review of a credible High dispute; proposed **five business days** for a decision or documented extension. These are **candidate service targets**, contingent on staffing, holiday coverage, escalation and legal approval. If not staffed, do not advertise or launch with them. Security/privacy exposure escalates to incident response immediately. Legal/public-claims owner approves the final SLA.
3. Review: compare original source snapshots permitted by license, current source availability, identity, independent lineages and contrary evidence. Require reviewer identity, case chronology and second-person approval before republishing High after a credible dispute. If evidence or display right fails, promptly lower to supported category or suppress claim; do not retain a disputed High merely because a cache is fresh.
4. Resolution: notify complainant with plain-language result and appeal route; record old/new reason codes and public version; visibly note correction and date without revealing private dispute contents. Keep routine refreshes internal. Allow an appeal to a second reviewer. Record redaction, deletion and audit expiry under approved retention schedule.
