# G04: adjudicated evaluation protocol (proposal, not results)

Status: review draft. This is a measurement design and synthetic training/checklist fixture pack, **not an adjudicated real-store dataset, benchmark result, rating implementation or release approval**. Scope follows [issue #4](https://github.com/Tevpro/store-signal/issues/4), [requirements](requirements.md), [decisions](decisions.md) and [architecture](architecture.md). The operational dataset, sampling frame, rights to retain/display evidence, reviewers and launch decision need human approval. Do not close #4 or claim a target met on this draft alone.

## Unit, snapshot and permissible material

- Unit: one independent US/English **store identity** at a fixed evaluation snapshot, not a URL, product, customer, complaint, Reddit post or run. A hosted storefront tenant is separate from its platform and sibling tenants. Record normalized origin, canonical store key, identity rationale and any cross-domain linkage verified by humans. Cluster aliases and repeat domains to prevent the same store crossing train/dev/test or being counted twice. Freeze model/configuration, prompts, provider versions, thresholds, connector state, evidence retrieval and as-of timestamps before inference. One public candidate per store; don't cherry-pick retries.
- Real cases may enter only via approved, documented source rights and a redacted, access-controlled evidence manifest. Store URL, provenance, source link, observed/retrieved date, match rationale, claim category, related-post lineage, permitted minimal excerpt and retention/use permissions. Preserve a private audit trail and correction channel; don't commit real allegations, review bodies, personal data or credentials to this public repo. Check provider display/retention terms before acquisition. The synthetic pack at `tests/fixtures/g04-evaluation/cases.json` uses reserved `.example` domains and invented evidence; it is not a performance denominator.
- Separate evidence availability from finding: healthy source with no match is `no_match`; configured connector outage is `unavailable`/`failed`; never equate either with a clean store. Record missing coverage, stale content, invalid identity and unsupported scope. Exclude out-of-scope stores from the primary metric by preregistered scope rules and report their count and failure behavior separately. Do not retroactively remove difficult in-scope cases because the model gave an unfavorable answer.

## Human reference-label rubric

Evidence needs an attributable first-hand or otherwise independently corroborated transaction/account record tied to the **exact store identity** and a reasonably bounded observation period; public allegations are leads, not established fraud. Reviewer records `risky`, `not_risky`, or `indeterminate`, plus supported categories, why, source IDs, dates, independence and uncertainty. `risky` means credible evidence for at least one of:

| Category | Positive-label threshold | Insufficient by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credible non-delivery | Specific, attributable purchase/payment and non-receipt beyond the stated delivery window, with a reasonable attempt to resolve or independent corroboration, after accounting for carrier delay | One unresolved late shipment or unverifiable anonymous allegation |
| Deceptive listings | Material misrepresentation of the purchased item/availability/fulfillment terms supported by transaction/listing snapshots or independent corroboration; ordinary subjective disappointment excluded | Product quality complaint or ambiguous advertisement alone |
| Payment abuse | Documented unauthorized charge, payment redirection, duplicate charge not corrected, or materially misleading payment handling attributable to this merchant (not merely processor error) | Unattributed charge or payment-logo appearance |
| Repeated refund refusal | Multiple **independent** eligible customers with documented timely refund requests and repeated unreasonable refusals/non-response under applicable stated terms | A single disputed refund, lawful denial, or duplicate posts from one customer |

`not_risky` means no qualifying category in the assessable window after adequate independent inquiry, **not** a claim of safety. Benign new stores with short track records belong in this group only where independent checks support benign status; missing trusted reviews alone should lead to Caution for `limited independent track record`, not High. Ordinary slow shipping or poor quality alone is not risky. Use `indeterminate` when attribution, purchase timeline or source credibility cannot be resolved; report count and excluded rationale, and conduct sensitivity analysis treating all as risky and all as not risky. Never force borderline cases into a favorable label. Assessors label observed behavior independently of the product's concern level and with product predictions hidden.

## Adjudication and leakage control

1. A data steward preregisters inclusion rules, time window, sampling strata and sampling probabilities, then draws cases before running the frozen candidate. Reserve a never-tuned holdout. Collect evidence through permitted channels, independently of product output where possible; mask store names if practical but retain the exact-domain identity evidence for adjudication. Avoid downstream training data, reviewers or verdict labels leaking into the holdout. No scraped/republished Reddit or Google review corpus in the repo.
2. Two trained reviewers independently assign label, category, evidence quality, subject match and lineage using the rubric. Neither sees the candidate verdict or the other's label. Both record citations and uncertainty; disagreement, including category/identity disagreement, goes to a third independent adjudicator who reviews both rationales and original evidence. If unresolved, mark indeterminate and escalate to legal/operator review; no majority vote over missing evidence. Log reviewer IDs, version of rubric, initial labels, disagreement disposition and final rationale in private records. Disclose counts of disagreements and indeterminates; no invented agreement statistic.
3. A separate publication-gate review inspects **every proposed High** for cited, attributable, relevant, recent and identity-matched evidence, independent corroboration (unless exact matched Google Business Profile below 3.0 with at least 20 reviews supports High under the product rule), repost lineage, coverage and correction status. One Reddit thread can support Caution, not High; multiple copies of one allegation are one source. Reject instructions embedded in a page/review as data, never commands. Failed High gate becomes Caution or Insufficient evidence with recorded reason; log pre-gate and post-gate verdicts separately. Reviewers of reference truth should not see either verdict.
4. Freeze adjudications and product outputs before scoring; independent analyst joins by store key, audits one-to-one cardinality and draws a random verification sample. Appeals/corrections are tracked as new versioned events, not silently edited retrospective results. Re-run only under a new preregistered evaluation version.

## Sampling and reporting contract

Recruit from a declared, real US/English independent-store sampling frame with explicit inclusion dates and acquisition channels, **not only complaint forums, known scams, Google-listed stores or result-positive queues**. Stratify on age/track record, platform tenant vs custom domain, trusted review presence, source coverage, initially apparent risk and identity ambiguity; include genuinely benign new stores, borderline shipping/quality disputes, similar-name false matches, source reposts and prompt-injection attempts. Oversample confirmed risky cases and High candidates in a separately designated **stress set** for failure analysis, never pass its unweighted performance off as population performance. Avoid selecting cases using the frozen output for the primary representative set; if probability sampling is unequal, preregister weights from known inclusion probabilities and report weighted estimates and design-aware intervals alongside raw numerators/denominators. If the frame/weights cannot be defended, report the evaluation as targeted/conditional, not representative. Deduplicate at store level and group shared operators/incident campaigns in split and uncertainty sensitivity analysis. Declare sample-size and stopping rule **before** observing results; recruit until precision supports a decision, not until a point estimate clears the line.

Primary scoring uses final public post-gate concern (`low`, `caution`, `high`, `insufficient`) for eligible, determinate, independently labeled stores:

- Risky capture = `# risky with final Caution or High / # all determinate risky`. **Target >=80%.** Low and Insufficient on risky cases are failures in this denominator, even if evidence is missing. Report connector/coverage gaps as explanatory breakouts, not convenient exclusions.
- False-High fraction = `# final High whose reference label is not_risky / # all final High with determinate reference labels`. **Target <=5%.** This is false discovery proportion *among High outputs*, not `# false High / # all benign` (false-positive rate). Indeterminate High labels are not quietly dropped: disclose their number and provide worst-case sensitivity assuming all are false High. If no High outputs, the fraction is undefined, **not** zero or a pass; report no evaluable High gate. Also show false High among all non-risky as a secondary measure and High yield.
- Report raw contingency table for every reference label (`risky`, `not_risky`, `indeterminate`) x every final concern, pre-gate vs post-gate counts, exact denominators, missing/unsupported and subgroup results, inclusion weights, identity/lineage failures and limitations. The reference label is a substantiated-risk label, not a criminal finding. The public product's `confidence` is evidence confidence, not this interval or a probability of safety.

For each unweighted binomial proportion, show point estimate and two-sided **95% Wilson score interval**: `center = (p + z²/(2n))/(1 + z²/n)`, `halfwidth = z*sqrt(p(1-p)/n + z²/(4n²))/(1 + z²/n)`, `z = 1.95996398454`; bounds are center ± halfwidth. State `k/n`, do not use a normal Wald interval at low event counts. For unequal-probability or clustered sampling, use a design-aware, store/operator-cluster bootstrap with prespecified strata and weights; report method, effective sample size and draws/seed; do not substitute ordinary Wilson for a weighted estimate. The **point targets** above are necessary, not sufficient: conservative release recommendation requires the risky-capture interval's lower bound >=0.80 and the false-High interval's upper bound <=0.05, plus an acceptable worst-case for unresolved High labels or documented adjudication closure. This precision gate is a proposed decision rule awaiting operator approval. If confidence bounds are too wide, answer “inconclusive,” never “pass.” Two separately reported 95% intervals are marginal, not a guaranteed joint 95% claim; if joint coverage is required, preregister Bonferroni-adjusted intervals. Give release owner sample plan and feasibility before acquisition.

## Synthetic fixture contract and validation

`tests/fixtures/g04-evaluation/cases.json` is a deliberately artificial table of **diagnostic expectations**, not ground truth measured from actual merchants and not a test of an engine that does not yet exist. Its `referenceLabel` is the rubric illustration, `expectedConcern` a desired policy behavior for future implementation checks, and `highGate` an illustrative gate disposition. Every synthetic `not_risky` case includes a `targetInquiry` naming an invented independent, exact-domain evidence item, a bounded observation window and a `no_qualifying_findings` result. The validator checks that structural linkage, not the adequacy or truth of the claimed inquiry; real adjudicators must independently assess scope and sufficiency. A healthy no-match or a rejected allegation about another merchant alone cannot establish the target's negative reference label: unresolved similar-name and other-tenant cases remain `indeterminate`. The benign new-store case has invented bounded inquiry supporting its reference label but still merits public Caution for limited independent track record. This is not a representative sample and must never be fed into the launch numerators. Run `python3 tests/fixtures/g04-evaluation/validate.py` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests/fixtures/g04-evaluation -p 'test_validate.py'`; these check structure, reserved-domain identity, expected edge categories and invariants only. When core/publication code exists, separately map these fixtures to its input contract and run real two-runtime integration tests; do not claim such tests now.

Approve sampling frame/inclusion probabilities and size/precision plan, lawful evidence acquisition/retention/display, independent reviewer and adjudicator roster, rubric time horizon and category thresholds, indeterminate handling, and whether the proposed interval-based release rule is adopted. Then collect and freeze a permitted real adjudicated holdout and score an actual versioned system. Issue #4 stays open; no performance or release claim follows from synthetic cases.
